The Heart of ZE­HAR

Our Story

Rooted in heritage, inspired by beauty, created with love.

A Beautiful Beginning

More than a pastry. A feeling.

ZE­HAR was imagined as a place where the warmth of Arabian hospitality meets the delicate artistry of modern patisserie.

Inspired by fragrant gardens, treasured family traditions and the joy of gathering, we believe that every dessert deserves to be a memorable experience.

Our creations are an expression of thoughtful ingredients, delicate flavours and a love for beautiful details.

Our Inspiration

The Poetry of Arabian Heritage

From the beauty of traditional architecture to the richness of Middle Eastern ingredients, Arabian culture inspires the soul of ZE­HAR.

Pistachio, rose, saffron, honey and dates bring together flavours that feel familiar yet beautifully unexpected.

Our Craft

Made with Patience and Purpose

We believe that true luxury lives in the smallest details.

Every texture, flavour and finishing touch is thoughtfully considered. Our approach combines the precision of European pastry techniques with the warmth of Arabian inspiration.

What We Believe

The Philosophy Behind Every Creation

Three simple principles guide everything that carries the ZE­HAR name.

✧

Authenticity

Celebrating meaningful flavours, beautiful traditions and honest ingredients.

❀

Artistry

Treating every dessert as a small work of art, crafted with care.

◇

Hospitality

Creating moments of warmth, generosity and togetherness.

Our Promise

Every celebration deserves a little royalty.

A little sweetness, a little beauty and a memory worth keeping.
