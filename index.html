The Atelier

Where heritage meets modern restraint

ZE­HAR was founded on a simple belief: pastry can be both ceremonial and intimate. We draw from the quiet poetry of Arabian gardens, the precision of European technique, and the warmth of shared tables.

Every creation begins with seasonal produce, single-origin nuts, and floral essences chosen for their purity. The result is a collection that feels editorial, organic, and timeless.
